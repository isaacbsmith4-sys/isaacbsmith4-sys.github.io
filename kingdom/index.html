How to post or schedule
  1. Pick a post type at the top and fill in the boxes. The picture updates as you type.
  2. Tap Save image, then Save Image in the menu that pops up. It goes to your Photos.
  3. Tap Copy caption.
  4. Open Instagram, tap +, pick the picture, and paste the caption.
  5. To schedule it for later (free): on the last screen tap More options → Schedule this post and pick a day and time. Or use the free Meta Business Suite app → Create post → Schedule.

Your photos stay on your phone. Nothing is posted until you post it.

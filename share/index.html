Posting tips
  • Post the card before the first game. A pick posted after tip-off is worth nothing to followers.
  • Post the recap every day, losing days too. The live record is the whole pitch, and people trust it because the losses are in it.
  • Only quote the live record. The app's season numbers include plays backfilled from before Oct 19, so never post those as results.
  • Keep the 21+ / 1-800-GAMBLER line. Instagram and TikTok restrict gambling content, so expect lower reach there than on X.
  • Never promise wins or "locks". If you ever sell picks or use sportsbook affiliate links, you have to disclose that, and the rules vary by state.

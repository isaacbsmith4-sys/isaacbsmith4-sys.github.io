Posting tips
  • Post plays before the game starts. A pick posted after tip-off is worth nothing to followers.
  • Post the recap every day, losing days too. People trust a record when they can see the losses in it.
  • Season includes backtested plays, so the line says "incl. backtest". Backtested plays were picked by the model after the games, not posted beforehand. If someone checks your old posts and those plays aren't there, the label is what keeps you honest. Live counts only plays posted at the time (since Oct 19).
  • Never promise wins or "locks". If you ever sell picks or use sportsbook affiliate links, you have to disclose that, and the rules vary by state.
